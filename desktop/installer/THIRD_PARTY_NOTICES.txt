Rhythm for Windows - third-party audio components

The installer contains separate library JARs, including components under
GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 (LGPL-2.1). The license text
is installed at licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt. These libraries are distributed
unmodified by the Rhythm project and are separate from Rhythm's own code.

Libraries identified in the build:
- FFSampledSP (com.tagtraum:ffsampledsp-complete:0.9.56), including its
  bundled FFmpeg decoding components. Project and source information:
  https://www.tagtraum.com/ffsampledsp/
- Jaudiotagger (net.jthink:jaudiotagger:3.0.1). Source:
  https://github.com/mioopp/jaudiotagger
- MP3SPI (com.googlecode.soundlibs:mp3spi:1.9.5.4), VorbisSPI
  (com.googlecode.soundlibs:vorbisspi:1.0.3.3), and Tritonus Share
  (com.googlecode.soundlibs:tritonus-share:0.3.7.4).
  Library binaries and version-specific source artifacts are available
  from their Maven Central artifact pages:
  https://search.maven.org/

The libraries are installed as separate JARs in Rhythm's app directory.
Recipients may inspect and replace these library files. Rhythm imposes no
additional restriction on modification of these LGPL components. This
notice does not imply endorsement by the library authors.

Other dependencies may carry their own notices within their JARs.
